Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Notice describes how ECOBRAL LLC handles information accessed through the ECOBRAL Amazon Ads API application (“the Application”).

1. Application purpose

The Application is an internal business tool used by ECOBRAL LLC to retrieve, organize, store, and analyze advertising information from ECOBRAL LLC’s own Amazon advertising accounts.

The Application is not offered as a public service and is not intended for use by customers, shoppers, or unrelated third parties.

2. Information accessed

With authorization from the applicable Amazon account, the Application may access advertising-related information including:

  • Advertising account and profile identifiers
  • Campaign and portfolio settings
  • Campaign names, statuses, budgets, and bidding strategies
  • Ad groups, keywords, match types, targets, and bids
  • Advertised products, ASINs, and SKUs
  • Impressions, clicks, advertising costs, sales, and purchases
  • Search-term and advertising-attribution information
  • Authentication tokens required to maintain authorized API access

3. How information is used

ECOBRAL LLC uses this information only for legitimate internal business purposes, including:

  • Monitoring advertising performance
  • Reviewing campaign settings and bid history
  • Calculating metrics such as CPC, ACoS, ROAS, and conversion rate
  • Preparing internal reports and dashboards
  • Improving advertising decisions and controlling advertising costs

4. Shopper and customer information

The Application is not designed to collect shoppers’ names, addresses, payment-card information, passwords, or other directly identifying customer information.

Advertising reports are used in the aggregated or account-level form provided through the Amazon Ads API.

5. Information sharing

ECOBRAL LLC does not sell, rent, or trade information obtained through the Application.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the Application, such as secure hosting, database, reporting, or development platforms. These providers receive access only when necessary to provide their services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect ECOBRAL LLC’s legal rights, systems, or accounts.

6. Data security

ECOBRAL LLC uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect advertising data and authentication credentials. Access is limited to authorized users and systems.

API credentials, client secrets, access tokens, and refresh tokens are not intentionally published in public source-code repositories.

7. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for advertising analysis, historical reporting, security, compliance, and legitimate business operations.

Authentication credentials and stored advertising data may be deleted when the Application is discontinued, authorization is revoked, or the information is no longer required.

8. Authorization and access revocation

Access to Amazon advertising information is based on authorization granted through Amazon. That authorization may be revoked through the applicable Amazon account or by contacting ECOBRAL LLC.

9. Changes to this notice

ECOBRAL LLC may update this Privacy Notice when the Application, applicable requirements, or data-handling practices change. The revised notice will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
