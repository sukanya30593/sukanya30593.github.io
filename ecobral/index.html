About us

ECOBRAL LLC is a United States ecommerce company that develops, markets, and sells consumer products through online marketplaces and direct-to-consumer channels.

Our business operates brands including RAAHI, offering botanical hair and skin oils and handcrafted home-fragrance products.

Amazon advertising technology

ECOBRAL LLC maintains an internal application for retrieving and analyzing advertising data from its own Amazon advertising accounts. The application supports campaign reporting, performance analysis, bid review, and advertising-cost management.

The application is used only for ECOBRAL LLC’s internal advertising operations and is not offered to outside advertisers or third parties.
